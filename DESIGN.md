**01 --- BRAND IDENTITY**

**1. Brand Identity**

**1.1 Brand Story**

OkilChai was built on a single belief: legal help should be as easy
to access as booking a doctor\'s appointment. The brand sits at the
intersection of authority and approachability --- serious enough to be
trusted with your most pressing legal matters, human enough to make the
process feel manageable.

**1.2 Brand Personality**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  **Trait**           **What It Means in Practice**

  **Trusted**         We behave like a respected senior partner ---
                      calm, credible, unshakeable under pressure.

  **Approachable**    We strip away legal intimidation. Plain
                      language, clear paths, zero jargon in the UI.

  **Precise**         Every pixel has a reason. Every word earns its
                      place. We do not waste the user\'s time.

  **Premium**         We look expensive --- not because we are
                      flashy, but because we are refined and
                      deliberate.

  **Empowering**      We hand power back to people who feel lost in
                      the legal system. We are their champion.

**02 --- LOGO SYSTEM**

**2. Logo System**

**2.1 Logo Construction**

The OkilChai logo is composed of two elements: the Scale Mark (icon)
and the Wordmark. They may be used together (lockup) or separately
depending on context.

> The scale icon represents balance, justice, and equality --- the core
> values of the legal system. The circular enclosure signals trust,
> completeness, and platform reliability.

**Primary Lockup --- Horizontal**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  **\[ HORIZONTAL LOCKUP \] Scale Icon + OkilChai wordmark**

  \[ ⚖ \] OkilChai

  Icon: 32px circle, gold scale on navy

  Font: Georgia Bold, 24px, Navy #0F1F3D

  Gap between icon and wordmark: 16px
  -----------------------------------------------------------------

**Primary Lockup --- Stacked**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  **\[ STACKED LOCKUP \] Icon above Wordmark**

  \[ ⚖ \]

  OkilChai

  Icon: 48px, centered above wordmark

  Font: Georgia Bold, 20px, Navy

  Use for: App icons, square thumbnails, favicons
  -----------------------------------------------------------------

**Icon Only (Monogram)**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  **\[ ICON ONLY \] Use only at 32px+ and when brand is already
  established**

  Navy background: Gold scale ⚖ on #0F1F3D

  Light background: Navy scale on #FAF8F4

  Minimum size: 32 x 32px

  Favicon: 16x16px simplified flat version
  -----------------------------------------------------------------

**2.2 Clear Space**

Always maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the
height of the capital \'L\' in the wordmark. Nothing --- no text, no
image edge, no other logo --- may enter this zone.

**2.3 Logo Don\'ts**

- Do not rotate or skew the logo in any direction

- Do not apply drop shadows, glows, or gradients to the logo

- Do not use the logo on a busy photographic background without a solid
  overlay

- Do not change any logo colors outside the approved palette

- Do not stretch or squish the logo disproportionately

- Do not place the gold logo on a white background --- use navy on white
  instead

- Do not add outlines or borders around the logo mark

**04 --- TYPOGRAPHY**

**4. Typography System**

OkilChai uses a two-family system: Playfair Display for all display
and heading copy (editorial authority), and DM Sans for all UI and body
text (modern clarity). This pairing creates productive tension ---
classic meets contemporary --- which perfectly mirrors the brand\'s
position.

> Web: Load both from Google Fonts. Fallback stack for headings:
> Georgia, \'Times New Roman\', serif. Fallback for body: -apple-system,
> system-ui, sans-serif.

**4.3 Typography Rules**

- Never use more than 2 typefaces on a single screen.

- Display headings (H1-H2) always use Playfair Display. Never DM Sans
  for hero text.

- Body copy line-height must always be 1.6 or greater for readability.

- Maximum line length (measure) for body text: 680px / \~70 characters.
  Never wider.

- Heading text on a navy background: always Gold (#C8A84B) or White.
  Never gray.

- Letter-spacing on uppercase labels: always +0.08em. On headings: 0 or
  -0.01em.

- Never use font-weight below 400 in body copy --- 300 Light is for
  large display only.

- Avoid justified text in web UI. Left-aligned body text only.

All spacing in OkilChai is based on a 4px base unit. All spacing
values are multiples of 4. This creates visual rhythm, alignment, and
consistency across the entire product.

> In Tailwind CSS, this maps exactly to the default spacing scale. In
> code: always use the spacing token --- never a raw pixel value.

**08 --- MOTION & ANIMATION**

**8. Motion & Animation**

Motion in OkilChai is purposeful, never decorative. Every animation
has a job: guide attention, confirm an action, communicate system state,
or reduce perceived load time.

**8.1 Core Principles**

- Responsive: Animations complete in under 300ms. Never block user
  interaction.

- Meaningful: Every animation has a semantic reason. No spinning for the
  sake of it.

- Accessible: Always respect prefers-reduced-motion. All animations
  degrade gracefully to instant transitions.

- Consistent: Use the defined easing curves and durations below ---
  never arbitrary values.

**8.2 Duration Scale**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  **Name / Duration** **Use Case**

  **instant / 0ms**   State changes that should feel immediate:
                      toggle, checkbox, radio

  **fast / 100ms**    Micro-interactions: button press, hover bg
                      color change

  **base / 150ms**    Default transition: most hover states, focus
                      rings, border changes

  **medium / 200ms**  Card hover lift, dropdown open, tooltip appear

  **slow / 300ms**    Modal appear, slide-in drawer, page transition

  **leisurely /       Page load stagger animations, skeleton to
  500ms**             content fade
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------

**8.3 Easing Curves**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  **Name**            **Value & Usage**

  **ease-standard**   cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1.0) --- Default for
                      most transitions

  **ease-enter**      cubic-bezier(0.0, 0, 0.2, 1.0) --- Elements
                      entering the screen (modals, drawers)

  **ease-exit**       cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1.0) --- Elements
                      leaving the screen

  **ease-bounce**     cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) ---
                      Confirmation success, reward moments only

  **ease-linear**     linear --- Progress bars, skeleton shimmer
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------

  **Subjects**        Diverse people (clients and lawyers) in real
                      conversations, not posed. Authentic over
                      perfect.

  **Color Grade**     Slight warm tone overlay --- cream highlights,
                      slightly desaturated shadows. Never
                      oversaturated.

  **Backgrounds**     Clean, uncluttered. Cream, white, soft bokeh
                      office environments.

  **Avoid**           Overly staged handshakes, gavel stock photos,
                      courthouse steps clichés, scale-of-justice
                      props.

  **Preferred**       Client on laptop at home, lawyer in a modern

**11 --- ACCESSIBILITY**

**11. Accessibility Standards**

OkilChai is committed to WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance across all
surfaces. Legal access should be truly universal.

**11.1 Color Contrast Requirements**

  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------
  **Combination**     **Ratio & Status**

  **Navy text on      13.1:1 --- PASS (AAA). Primary body text
  White**             combination.

  **White text on     13.1:1 --- PASS (AAA). All white-on-dark text.
  Navy**              

  **Gold on Navy**    3.2:1 --- PASS for large text (18px+ bold)
                      only. Not for body copy.

  **Gold text on      FAIL --- Never use gold text on white or cream
  White**             backgrounds.

  **Gray-600 on       5.9:1 --- PASS (AA). Secondary text
  White**             combination.

  **Gray-400 on       2.7:1 --- FAIL. Use Gray-600 minimum for body
  White**             text.

  **Error on White**  5.2:1 --- PASS (AA). Error messages.

  **Success on        4.8:1 --- PASS (AA). Success text.
  White**             
  ------------------- ----------------------------------------------

**11.2 Accessibility Requirements**

- All interactive elements must be keyboard focusable and operable. Tab
  order must be logical.

- Focus rings: 3px, Navy color, offset 2px --- never remove focus
  outlines.

- All images must have descriptive alt text. Decorative images:
  alt=\'\'.

- All form inputs must have associated \<label\> elements --- never
  placeholder-only.

- Error messages must be associated with inputs via aria-describedby.

- Lawyer profiles use structured data (JSON-LD LegalService schema) for
  screen readers and SEO.

- All modals must trap focus and return focus to trigger element on
  close.

- Skip navigation link at top of page for keyboard and screen reader
  users.

- Time-limited content (slot expiry timers) must announce updates via
  aria-live.

- Minimum touch target size: 44 x 44px on all interactive elements.
